# Tableau Tendance UI/UX Brief

This project is now for **Tableau Tendance**, a premium e-commerce website for selling decorative tableaux and wall art in Tunisia.

## Brand

- Name: Tableau Tendance
- Descriptor: Tableaux decoratifs
- Market: Tunisia
- Offer: Decorative canvas paintings, wall art, packs, and themed collections
- Payment: Cash on delivery
- Delivery: Everywhere in Tunisia

## Important Direction

The website does not need public service pages, boutique service descriptions, or reservation-first flows. All customer-facing pages should prioritize product discovery and sales.

Remove or avoid:

- Tableau Tendance branding
- Beauty boutique copy
- Service booking language
- Appointment CTAs
- Service-duration pricing
- Boutique-focused gallery wording

Use instead:

- Tableau references such as AB01, AB02, AB03
- Product categories like Modernes, Calligraphie, Nature, Abstrait, Animaux, Paysages
- Cart and order CTAs
- WhatsApp support for product questions
- Trust messaging around delivery, payment on delivery, and premium quality

## Visual Direction

The design should feel premium, warm, and modern:

- Background: warm cream
- Primary dark: black / dark brown
- Accent: gold
- Product cards: clean white cards with restrained borders
- Typography: elegant serif headings and modern sans-serif body text
- Imagery: interiors, decorative wall art, canvas paintings, warm living rooms

## Home Page Sections

The home page should lead with commerce:

1. Hero: “Sublimez Votre Interieur”
2. Trust pills: delivery, cash on delivery, premium quality
3. Collections: modern, calligraphy, nature, abstract, animals, offers
4. Promotional banner: Pack 4 Tableaux at 99 DT
5. Popular products
6. About Tableau Tendance
7. Contact / WhatsApp support

## Public Navigation

Recommended public navigation:

- Accueil
- Nos tableaux
- Collections
- Galerie
- Contact
- Panier

The admin/dashboard area may still contain existing operational tools, but the public storefront should read as a tableau sales website.
